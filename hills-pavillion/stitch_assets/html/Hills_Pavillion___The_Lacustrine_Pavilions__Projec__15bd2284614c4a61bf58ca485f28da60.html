ARCHITECTURAL CREED & PROVENANCE

DESIGNED AROUND THE WAY PEOPLE LIVE.

The Lacustrine Pavilions emerge directly from the granite bedrock of the Upper Engadin. Conceived in dialogue with the crystalline glacial air, each pavilion balances absolute monolithic massing against panoramic apertures that dissolve the threshold between alpine interior and pristine water.

Hand-planed local larch, honed Dorato stone, and raw aggregate concrete establish a tactile resonance with the surrounding pine forests. Subterranean climate galleries seamlessly tuck automotive storage beneath the earth, leaving the natural slope untouched and fully restored.

Engineered for generational preservation, the estate leverages 1,200-meter closed-loop geothermal bores coupled with tri-layer acoustic glazing, achieving Net-Zero operating energy during Alpine winters down to −28°C.
